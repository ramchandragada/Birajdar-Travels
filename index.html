Children Pricing Policy

0 – 5 years Free

No package charge. Infants not counted in adult pricing tier.

5 – 12 years 50% charge

Half of the per-person rate for your adult group size (e.g. 50% of ₹10,000 = ₹5,000).

12 years & above Full person

Counted as an adult traveller for vehicle allocation and package pricing.

Example: 2 adults + 1 child (8 yrs) in Hatchback = ₹20,000 + 50% of ₹10,000 = ₹25,000 total.
